Last updated: July 16, 2026

This policy explains how Ura Click handles information when you use the app.

Information and app operation

Ura Click does not require an account or ask for your name or email address inside the app. Practice settings and presets are stored on your device to operate the app.

Third-party services may process technical information, purchase status, product identifiers, advertising identifiers, ad interaction data, and diagnostics as needed to provide their services.

Advertising and purchases

Google AdMob displays banner and interstitial ads to users without the Pro entitlement. Ads are not shown when the Pro entitlement is active.

Pro is a one-time Apple in-app purchase. RevenueCat helps manage purchase status, entitlement status, and restoration. Apple processes payment information.
