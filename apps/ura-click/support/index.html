Ura Click - Offbeat Metronome

Ura Click helps musicians practise steady timing, frontbeat and offbeat clicks, time signatures, BPM changes, and bar-based switching exercises.

Frequently asked questions

What does Pro include?

Pro is a one-time, non-consumable in-app purchase. It removes ads and enables Pro features such as preset saving where available.

How do I restore a purchase?

Open the Pro area in the app and use the restore button with the Apple ID used for the original purchase.

Why is there no sound?

  • Check device volume, Silent Mode, and Focus settings.
  • Stop and restart playback.
  • Restart the app if the audio session does not recover.

How do I contact support?

Replace your-email@example.com with the support address before publishing this site. Ask users to include their device model, iOS version, and a short description of the issue.
